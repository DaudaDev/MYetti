Lives Changed, Futures Built

Behind every scholarship is a story — of a family that believed, a student who persevered, and a community that rose together. These are the words of those MYET has had the honour of walking alongside.

“
On behalf of the family of Mal Haruna Sabo and the entire ECWA Church Yantamma, I write to express our deepest gratitude for your generous support, prompt assistance, and the mentorship programme that has made a profound impact on my studies.

Your support has not only relieved a heavy financial burden, it has given me the confidence to focus on my education with ease and purpose. The mentorship has equipped me with essential skills and insights — preparing me for my future career and strengthening my stand in the faith as a Hausa Christian student.

Your unwavering commitment to students like me is truly inspiring. I am taking up the challenge to one day do the same for others. May the Lord bless every person who contributed to this act of kindness and enlarge their coasts in every area of endeavour.

Zakariya Barau Sabo

Bachelor of Dental Surgery — Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto

“
I want to take a moment to express my heartfelt gratitude to the MYET Board for your generosity in supporting my education. Your investment in my future has made a lasting impact on my academic journey, and I will forever be grateful.

Your support has not only eased financial burdens — it has instilled in me the confidence to pursue my goals. I am committed to making the most of this opportunity and ensuring that your trust in me is well placed.

Thank you for believing in me and contributing to my success. I am honoured to have you as a part of my educational journey. God bless you all.

MYET Sponsored Student
