Three Ways You Can Change a Life Today

Every MYET scholar is the result of someone refusing to watch from the sidelines. Choose the path that fits — your impact is immediate, transparent, and lasting.

Sponsor a Student

Commit to funding one scholar's tuition, books, and mentorship for a full academic year. You receive progress reports, photos and the knowledge that one child stays in school because of you.

From ₦150,000 / year · Direct, trackable, receipted

One-off Donation

Give any amount, any time. 100% of your gift goes into the scholarship fund — every naira is receipted and tied to a named line item in our audited accounts.

Any amount · CAC-registered · Fully accounted

Donate Now

Become a Mentor

Offer one hour a month. Professionals in medicine, engineering, theology, law, business or public service can change a scholar's trajectory more than tuition ever could.

~1 hour / month · Structured pairing · Training provided

Why You Can Trust MYET

We are a community-founded, community-governed trust — built on the same principles we teach our scholars.

CAC-Registered Trust

A formally registered Nigerian Trust under the Corporate Affairs Commission.

Board of Trustees

Governed by a 12-member Board meeting bi-monthly. See the full roster on our How We Work page.

Transparent Accounting

Every naira is tracked line-by-line. Donors receive reports on the scholars their gift supports.

10 Years of Impact

Over 120 Masihiyawa students sponsored across Kebbi, Sokoto and Zamfara since founding.
